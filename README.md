# Virtual Cleanroom Experience

## Overview

As part of the **Semiconductor Fabrication 101** course offered by **Purdue University**, I explored virtual semiconductor fabrication equipment and cleanroom workflows using **VFabLab**. This experience provided an introduction to the equipment, process sequences, and wafer-handling procedures involved in semiconductor manufacturing through a virtual cleanroom environment.

## Processes Explored

The following semiconductor fabrication processes were explored through the virtual cleanroom:

- Atomic Layer Deposition (ALD)
- Wet Etching
- Dry Etching
- Photolithography
- Sputtering
- Oxidation
- Chemical Mechanical Planarization (CMP)

## Learning Outcomes

- Gained virtual exposure to semiconductor fabrication equipment and cleanroom environments.
- Learned how wafers are physically handled and transferred between different processing tools.
- Developed an understanding of the sequence of operations involved in wafer fabrication.
- Became familiar with software-guided equipment operations and the procedural steps associated with different fabrication processes.
- Improved my understanding of how individual fabrication steps contribute to the overall semiconductor manufacturing workflow.

## Course and Platform

- **Course:** Semiconductor Fabrication 101
- **Institution:** Purdue University
- **Virtual Learning Platform:** [VFabLab](https://vfablab.org/)

## Scope of Experience

This experience focused on virtual equipment familiarization, wafer-handling workflows, and software-guided fabrication procedures. It provided introductory exposure to semiconductor manufacturing processes without involving the physical operation of fabrication equipment or the processing of actual wafers.
